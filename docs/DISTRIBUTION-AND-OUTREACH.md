## 2. Cold-Call Phone Scripts

### Script A: The "Night Test" (Target: Clinics, Salons, High-Ticket Professional Services)
**Angle:** The business is losing 30–40% of its high-value customer inquiries because nobody answers after 6:00 PM or during peak lunch rushes.

> **Caller (Rep):** "Hi [Owner Name / Clinic Director], my name is [Rep Name] with Helix AI in [Dubai / Riyadh / Cairo]. I'm calling with a quick 30-second question—have you ever called your own clinic line at 8:30 in the evening to see what a new patient hears?"
>
> **Prospect:** "No, why? We have an answering machine / we're closed then."
>
> **Caller:** "Right. What happens right now in [Dubai / Riyadh] is that patients searching for dental or specialty appointments after hours simply hang up and book with whoever answers first.
>
> We just deployed an autonomous bilingual voice receptionist for clinics across [UAE / KSA] that answers on the second ring in natural Gulf Arabic or English, checks your real-time Cal.com calendar, confirms the slot, and sends an automated WhatsApp itinerary with your Google Maps pin before they hang up.
>
> We set up a live interactive sandbox where you can hear the Arabic voice and test your own clinic's branding in 60 seconds:
> `https://helixai.com/dashboard/studio`
>
> Would you be open to me sending that link directly to your WhatsApp so you can test it on your own phone?"

### Script B: The "5-Second Rescue" (Target: Contractors, Emergency Maintenance, Auto, Home Services)
**Angle:** Missed calls = lost cash. In emergency plumbing, HVAC, and roadside assistance, callers dial the next contractor immediately.

> **Caller (Rep):** "Hi [Owner Name], this is [Rep Name] from Helix AI. I won't waste your time—we ran an audit on emergency service calls in [Amman / Cairo / Dubai] last week. When a technician is on a ladder or driving, 7 out of 10 incoming calls go to voicemail, and over 90% of those callers hire a competitor within 3 minutes.
>
> What we do is very simple: the second your line rings out or you miss a call, our AI fires a WhatsApp to that caller in under 4 seconds: *'Hi, we saw we just missed your call—what emergency service do you need help with right now?'*
>
> It collects their address pin, confirms the problem, and pings your WhatsApp queue with the job ready to dispatch.
>
> Can I send you a 10-second test trigger so you can see how fast it hits your phone?"
